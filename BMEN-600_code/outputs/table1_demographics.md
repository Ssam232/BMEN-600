### Group comparisons (p-values)

| Comparison | Age | Sex | CSF availability |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|

**Notes**

- Age is age at the year-1 MRI scan, as recorded in IDA. Percentages are of each column's n.
- "All controls" is every control kept. All 78 are matched to MCI participants, and 47 of them are also matched to AD participants, so the control columns overlap.
- Total sample counts each person once (24 AD + 39 MCI + 78 controls = 141).
- "CSF available" means the participant has at least one Roche Elecsys CSF result (UPENNBIOMK_ROCHE_ELECSYS, any visit).
- Tests: age, Welch t-test for pairwise comparisons and one-way ANOVA across the three groups; sex and CSF availability, Fisher exact test for pairwise comparisons and chi-square across the three groups.
- MCI participants with CSF results were prioritised when controls ran out, so all kept MCI participants have CSF.
